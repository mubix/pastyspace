Meet the features
The real app, with a little sample cargo.
1

Find your space.

Pick a URL, or let PastySpace choose three random words. If the address is free, it’s yours to claim.

2

Drop something in.

A snippet, a screenshot, a stack of files. Add an optional title and choose how your text should look.

3

Lock it. Link it.

Save once and share the URL. The app won’t let anyone edit or overwrite your paste afterward.

Also speaks
fluent curl.

Already in a terminal? Stay there. Pipe in some text or send a file, and PastySpace hands you a URL.
